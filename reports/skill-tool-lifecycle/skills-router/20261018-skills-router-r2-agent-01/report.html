  • Major SKILL.md step 3 tells Claude that `conf: low` on a provider hint means BM25 and the engine disagreed. It does not. label() (route.mjs:423-428) also returns 'low' when min(confidence or stability, fit) < 0.35, and agreement defaults to 'off' (DEFAULT_THRESHOLDS, and jev.json does not set it). So with the shipped config, `conf: low` never means disagreement. The old text gated this on `agreement.agree: false`, and the line drops that field. Also, `(the engine's first, in alt)` has it backwards: finalize (route.mjs:362) puts the engine's top in `suggest:` and BM25's top in alt[0]. Claude will misread the hint. Fix: emit a field such as `agree: no` (or `conf: low (disagree)`) when agreement.agree === false, and reword the bullet to match. .claude/skills/skills-router/SKILL.md:61 · fixed: conf wording rewritten (low = uncertain score or disagreement); commit ada217d
