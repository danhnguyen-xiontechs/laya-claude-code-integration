skills-router: plain Claude vs Claude + JEV

REWORK · Fix first, then re-measure (confidence LOW)

On 6 pick-a-skill requests (4 with a skill as the answer, 2 needing none) with Haiku 5.5, plain Claude (A) with the full skill list picked right 12 of 12 times and Claude + the router hint (B, Jev live) also 12 of 12, so the hint changed no answer, while B used 72% more tokens (1.49M -> 2.56M) and about twice the turns (25 -> 44); no case got worse. The router itself was right on all 6 requests (0 Jev errors, ~0.8 s per call), but the cases were too easy to show value, and 3 Major tool findings stay open. Top fix: print the hint as one short line instead of a 2k-token JSON after a 30 KB list.
