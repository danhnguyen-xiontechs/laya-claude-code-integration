I fixed src/map-limit.js and `node --test` passed 4/4 on 11 runs. There were four root causes. The loop used `running <= limit`, which allowed limit+1 calls at once. It also did `results.push` in completion order instead of `results[i] = v`. Empty input never resolved. After a rejection, in-flight calls could still launch new ones. I also made a synchronous throw in `fn` reject, and clamped `limit` to at least 1. I extended test/map-limit.test.mjs with checks for order, peak concurrency equal to the limit, empty input, and first-error-then-no-new-calls.
