Created src/pages.js in the work folder (CommonJS, exports `pageWindow` via module.exports). It validates page (integer 1..totalPages, else RangeError `page out of range: <page>`), takes len = min(span, totalPages) pages starting at page - floor(span/2), and clamps the window to 1..totalPages. `node check.mjs` prints PASS. Extra checks: pageWindow(100,10,10,4) gives [7,8,9,10] and pageWindow(0,1,10,3) gives [1].
