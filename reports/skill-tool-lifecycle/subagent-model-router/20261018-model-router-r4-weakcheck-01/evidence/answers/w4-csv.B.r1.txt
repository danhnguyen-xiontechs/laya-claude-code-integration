Created src/csv.js in the work folder (CommonJS, exports parseCsv, with no dependencies) implementing the RFC-style rules in the prompt, including quoted fields with embedded commas, LF/CR and doubled quotes, LF/CRLF records, no extra record for a trailing newline, [] for empty input, and the "unterminated quoted field" error. `node check.mjs` prints PASS, and extra edge cases I ran (quoted empty field, empty middle line, CRLF, embedded newlines) all matched the spec.
